We build AI workforces — not features.

There's a difference between a chatbot and an agent. A chatbot answers questions. An agent takes action: it books, sells, recovers, ranks, writes, and calls — without being told to.

Our five agents (Ranko, Quill, Vega, Iris, and Otto) are purpose-built for the jobs that SMBs pay humans to do badly at 2am. We automate the entire workflow, not just the easy part of it.

We operate across the UK, US and UAE — three of the world's most competitive SMB markets. We built TechWon for businesses that move fast, expect results in days, and can't afford to wait for enterprise procurement cycles.

· What we believe ·

Built on three principles.

Everything we ship comes back to these three ideas.

⚡

Action over advice

We don't deliver reports and recommendations. We deliver running agents. The result is a live system that takes action in your business, not a slide deck about what you should do.

🔍

Radical transparency

Every interaction every agent makes is logged and visible. You see every call Iris handles, every cart Vega recovers, every rank Ranko moves. No black boxes, ever.

🤝

SMB-first pricing

Enterprise AI pricing is designed to exclude small businesses. Ours isn't. We priced our agents so a dental clinic in London, a restaurant in Dubai or an e-commerce store in New York can afford them on day one.

🚀

7-day live promise

Most AI deployments take months. Ours take a week. We've built the infrastructure to move fast without cutting corners — discovery to live agent in 7 days or less.

🌐

Built for global markets

English, Arabic, French and 19 more — our agents are built for the languages and business rhythms of the UK, US and UAE, and every other market you want to reach.

📈

Revenue-first metrics

We don't report vanity metrics. We report revenue recovered, calls handled, leads booked and rankings moved. If it doesn't move money, it doesn't make our dashboard.
