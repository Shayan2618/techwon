Common questions

How long does setup take?

Most clients are live within 7 days. Complex multi-location setups take up to 14 days. We handle all configuration — your team just reviews and approves workflows.

Do I need to change my existing tools?

No. Our agents plug into your existing stack — Shopify, WooCommerce, Calendly, Google Calendar, Stripe, custom APIs. We build the bridge, not a replacement.

What if I only need one agent?

Totally fine. You can start with a single agent (e.g. Iris for call handling) and add more as you see results. Agents are modular and independent.

Is there a contract or lock-in?

All plans are month-to-month. You can pause or cancel with 30 days notice. We keep clients by delivering results, not by legal obligation.

What languages do the agents support?

Iris (voice) supports 22 languages and switches mid-call. All text-based agents support English, Arabic, Urdu, Spanish, French and 12 more — just tell us what you need.

How do I see what the agents are doing?

Every agent comes with a live dashboard and weekly automated reports. You see every interaction, outcome, and metric. No black boxes.
